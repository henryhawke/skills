# Mockup Prompt Recipes

## Master Prompt

Use this base for every screen and append one screen recipe below.

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity screen-by-screen mobile UI presentation board
Input images: Image 1 is a style reference, not an edit target
Primary request: Art-direct Fart With Friends as a precision social instrument manufactured by a deranged candy laboratory: 80% authored product, 20% toy. Preserve real product contracts while replacing neon UI-kit repetition with route-specific silhouette, editorial typography, asymmetric rhythm, controlled atmosphere, and one unmistakably FWF signature moment.
Scene/backdrop: near-black matte plum studio stage with one directional spotlight, subtle fixed mesh, faint structural grid, nearly invisible monochrome grain, and a dark quiet perimeter; no fog, stars, bubbles, particles, or loose sticker wallpaper
Style/medium: realistic shippable mobile product UI, not concept art; straight-on modern phone; matte plum stage, quiet acrylic structure, printed sticker punctuation, inflated gel only for the app-logo gas action
Color palette: 70% night plum #12091D, candy white #FFF8FF and muted lilac #CBB9D5, methane lime #A8F04F for active gas actions, bubblegum pink #FF70AE for social/reaction, cyan #BDF3F8 for information, yellow #FFE45B for recording/warning
Typography: Archivo Expanded used as a spatial object for one rare hero phrase or oversized timer; Public Sans for readable UI; strong scale contrast and purposeful line breaks; sentence-case actions; no glow, chrome, outline, or distortion on body text
Composition/framing: one complete primary phone with an asymmetric 60/40 internal composition and a route-specific silhouette, plus at most two smaller state closeups; rigid 8dp underlying grid with one deliberate content-layer axis break; alternate spacious hero, compressed information band, and quiet utility layer
Materials/textures: restrained tonal acrylic without universal borders, directional gel lighting only on the app-logo hero, flat printed sticker punctuation, real waveform fingerprints; maximum three containment zones
Constraints: use assets/images/icon.png as the canonical Fart action mark; preserve the verified screen contract; 44–48px touch targets; 8dp grid; 80px center action where applicable; one hero and at most one signature moment; readable exact labels; portfolio-grade, accessible, performant, and buildable
Avoid: centered title + centered hero + centered CTA repetition, rainbow border-per-card, anime avatars, generic wind/cloud fart glyph, generic cyberpunk HUD, random stickers, decorative waveforms, smoke, star fields, animated blobs, fake microtext, universal gloss/glow, watermarks
```

## 1. Inbox and Friends

Add: Main screen title `INBOX`; segmented control `Inbox | Friends`; unread badge; expand the newest actionable fart into an editorial lead row with a stable waveform fingerprint and optional reaction frame; flatten older fart/reply/reaction items into quiet rows without colored outline-per-item. Smaller state shows `FRIENDS` with search and requests. Keep exact shell navigation and app-logo center action.

Exact text: `INBOX`, `Inbox`, `Friends`, `New`, `Reaction clip`, `Search friends`, `Requests`.

## 2. Composer

Add: Three states on one board. Primary phone uses the actual app logo as a tactile Toxic Seal dominating the lower two-thirds, an offset contact-driven Pressure Halo, oversized tabular timer `0:07 / 0:10`, and a real waveform escaping slightly into the stage. Smaller states show idle tap/hold guidance and the destination-addressing sheet with `Private`, `Fart Map`, and `Edit`. Private has a selected recipient; Fart Map clearly says public and location required.

Exact text: `Tap to send · Hold to record`, `0:07 / 0:10`, `Choose where it goes`, `Private`, `Fart Map`, `Edit`, `Public · Location required`.

## 3. Incoming and Reaction

Add: Primary phone shows the active hold state as one cinematic audio-camera composition: actual app-logo press target, audio progress halo, live front-camera preview, red/yellow `Recording reaction` state, `Private until you send`, release-to-stop guidance, sender/distance/delivery, plus `Save` and `Fart back`. State closeups show pre-listen and a large clean reaction preview with `None`, `Slow-mo`, `High-pitch`, `Discard`, `Listen again`, and the only filled action `Send reaction`.

Exact text: `HOLD TO LISTEN`, `Friend fart`, `Save`, `Fart back`, `Reaction ready`, `None`, `Slow-mo`, `High-pitch`, `Discard`, `Listen again`, `Send reaction`.

## 4. Fart Map and Detail

Add: Primary phone is a real city map titled `FART MAP` with public marker clusters, nearby/walked-through/tracking metrics, recenter/refresh/fullscreen controls, `Everyone | My friends`, and `LIVE`. Both filters remain public-only. No public/private toggle. Smaller state shows a selected public drop with audio playback, time, listens, optional note, `Public Map`, `Share`, `Fart back`, and `Refresh`.

Exact text: `FART MAP`, `Public drops only`, `Nearby`, `Walked through`, `Tracking`, `LIVE`, `Public Map`, `Share`, `Fart back`, `Refresh`.

## 5. Saved

Add: Primary phone is a two-column audio collection titled `SAVED`; use waveform art and typography as cover identity, varied but disciplined tile composition, and a compact stats/filter rail. Portraits do not dominate every tile and there are no rainbow semantic borders. Unavailable items stay structurally present but de-energized. Smaller state shows filter and select overlays with rename, highlight, share, and remove.

Exact text: `SAVED`, `All`, `Public only`, `Sort`, `Rename`, `Highlight`, `Share`, `Remove`, `Unavailable`.

## 6. Onboarding

Add: A refined four-frame sequence on one board: `PICK YOUR FART TAG` with availability and suggestions; tutorial spotlight on the center Fart button; reaction consent with camera/mic explanation and `Not now`; first-fart handoff with tap/hold guidance. Keep screens simple and progressive.

Exact text: `PICK YOUR FART TAG`, `Available`, `Try another`, `THIS IS THE FART BUTTON`, `Enable reactions?`, `Not now`, `Enable`, `SEND YOUR FIRST FART`, `Tap to send · Hold to record`.

## 7. Profile

Add: Primary phone shows an identity-led asymmetric profile: large portrait crop, strong Fart Tag typography, compact Farts/Friends/Walked Through ribbon, then calm flat rows for friends, invite/share, notifications, and settings. Smaller state shows another user with a visually neutral safety menu for unfriend, block, and report. Keep the screen personal, not gamified.

Exact text: `PROFILE`, `Farts`, `Friends`, `Walked Through`, `Invite friends`, `Share profile`, `Notifications`, `Settings`, `Unfriend`, `Block`, `Report`.
