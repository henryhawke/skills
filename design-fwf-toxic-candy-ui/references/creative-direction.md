# Creative Direction

Design a **precision social instrument manufactured by a deranged candy laboratory**. Target 80% authored product and 20% toy.

## Authorship

- Give every route a recognizable silhouette.
- Keep an 8dp grid but allow one deliberate content-layer axis break.
- Use asymmetric 60/40 balance when content permits.
- Alternate spacious hero, compressed information band, and quiet utility layer.
- Remove containment before adding decoration; ordinary screens get at most three major zones.
- Avoid repeating centered title + centered hero + centered CTA across routes.

## Typography

- Use Archivo Expanded as a spatial object at 36–44sp for rare hero copy.
- Keep route titles 22–28sp, functional copy 16–17sp, metadata 12–13sp.
- Reserve uppercase for route authority and recording/status stamps.
- Use oversized tabular timers and real waveform fingerprints as composition.
- Never outline, distort, chrome, or glow body text.

## Atmosphere

- Use one directional light and one temperature per screen: cyan map, pink social/reaction, yellow recording, lime gas action.
- Keep the perimeter dark; use subtle fixed mesh/spotlight, structural grid, and nearly invisible grain.
- No stars, smoke, fog, wallpaper particles, idle blobs, or decorative waveforms.
- Reduced transparency becomes matte; reduced motion removes directional energy.

## Signature moments

- Toxic Seal: the app logo as the canonical gel action.
- Audio Fingerprint: stable waveform identity across Inbox, Saved, Map, and Listen.
- Pressure Halo: contact-driven hold progress and subtle gel compression.
- Send Trajectory: an audio token travels to friend or public-map destination.
- Inbox-to-Listen and Marker-to-Sheet morphs preserve spatial origin.
- Reaction Flash Frame: hold the final local frame briefly before preview; never auto-send.

Use only one signature moment at a time. Borrow Awwwards-style authorship, typography, atmosphere, and choreography—not scroll hijacking, navigation novelty, cursor tricks, perpetual WebGL, or performance debt.
